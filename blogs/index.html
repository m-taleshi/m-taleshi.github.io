PhD Defense Oct 2025

🎓 Successfully Defended PhD Thesis

Defended my doctoral dissertation "Sensitivity of High-Fidelity Neural Interfaces to Perturbations" at Aalto University School of Electrical Engineering. The thesis investigates how reliable non-invasive neural interfaces based on high-density surface EMG and motor-unit decoding are when real-world disturbances occur.

Opponent: Prof. Utku Ş Yavuz, University of Twente, The Netherlands

Custos: Prof. Ivan Vujaklija, Aalto University
